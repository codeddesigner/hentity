Help & guidelines

How launching on Hentity works

One board a week, up to 6 launches a day, ranked by the community. These are the rules that keep it fair.

Who can launch

  • Software, apps, APIs, AI tools, developer tools, hardware and infrastructure that people can use today.
  • Built by African founders or teams, or with significant development in Africa.
  • Not eligible: agencies, basic company sites, and concepts that aren’t live yet.

Launch days and the weekly board

  • Makers pick their own launch day, up to 5 weeks ahead, or a past day if the product already launched.
  • You go live at 06:00 WAT on your day and stay on that week’s board until it resets on Monday at 06:00 WAT.
  • The top 3 become Products of the Week, with medals and badges for their sites.

Votes

  • Ask for support and feedback, not upvotes. Coordinated voting is flagged, and those votes stop counting.
  • Votes from new or flagged accounts count for less, so rank can differ from the raw count.
  • Rankings can’t be bought. Sponsored spots are always labelled “Promoted”.

Reviews

A moderator checks that the product is real, working and has an African connection. Most reviews finish within about 1 hour, and resubmissions after requested changes are just as quick.
